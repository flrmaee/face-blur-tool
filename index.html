          About Face Blur Tool

          Face Blur Tool helps you share photos responsibly. It finds faces with an on-device AI model and covers them with the style you choose, so you decide what stays visible.

          • Local processing. Your photos are decoded, analysed and edited in your browser's memory. They are never uploaded to a server, saved in a database or written to browser storage. (The detection model files are downloaded once from a public CDN; your photos are not part of that.)
          • You stay in control. Detection is a helper, not a guarantee. It can miss small, turned or covered faces. Review the result and use the manual tool for anything else you want hidden.
          • Closing the tab clears everything. There is nothing to delete afterwards.
